Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

In short: ClipperBOT is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. We do not run servers that collect your data, we have no user accounts, and we do not sell or share your information. Your settings, clips and connected-account tokens stay on your computer. ClipperBOT only sends data to the services you choose to use, and only so it can do what you asked.

Who we are

ClipperBOT ("the app", "we") is a desktop application for Windows and macOS that finds highlight moments in Twitch streams, turns them into short vertical videos, and, when you choose, uploads them to your own social media accounts. Questions about this policy can be asked at github.com/ScuttleK/clipperbot-site/issues.

What stays on your computer

Everything ClipperBOT stores is kept in its data folder on your computer:

  • Your settings, including API keys and access tokens for accounts you connect.
  • Short recordings of the public Twitch streams you choose to monitor, the clips made from them, their transcripts, and the public chat messages sent during those moments.
  • Your review decisions (approve / reject), which the app uses on your computer to learn what you like.
  • Log files that help you troubleshoot the app.

We (the developers) never receive any of this. You can delete all of it at any time by deleting the data folder.

Information from TikTok and other platforms you connect

When you connect a TikTok account (or YouTube, Instagram or Facebook), you sign in on that platform's own page and approve access. ClipperBOT then receives and stores on your computer:

  • An access token and refresh token, used only to upload the videos you approve to that account.
  • Your account's display name (TikTok scope user.info.basic), shown in the app so you can see which account is connected.

ClipperBOT uses the TikTok scopes video.upload and video.publish only to upload or publish videos that you approved, with the title, caption and privacy setting shown to you in the app. It does not read your followers, messages, other videos or any other account data, and it does not post anything you didn't approve (automatic posting is off unless you turn it on and is limited by the daily limits you set).

These tokens are never sent anywhere except back to the platform that issued them. You can disconnect an account in ClipperBOT's Publishing page at any time, and you can also revoke ClipperBOT's access from that platform's own settings (on TikTok, in the security and permissions section of Settings and privacy, where apps with access to your account are listed).

Services ClipperBOT talks to

Depending on the features you use, the app sends data directly from your computer to:

  • Twitch: to find live streams, watch the streams you monitor and read their public chat. Uses the Twitch API credentials you enter.
  • TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook: only if you connect them, to upload the clips you approve.
  • Anthropic (Claude): only if you turn on the optional Claude clip judge and enter your own Anthropic API key. For each detected moment it sends still frames, the transcript and the chat messages (with chat usernames replaced by placeholders) so Claude can score the moment and write a title. This is processed under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy on your API account.
  • GitHub: to check for and download app updates.
  • Hugging Face: to download the speech-recognition model the first time it is needed.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy.

Children

ClipperBOT is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

All connections to the services above use HTTPS. The app's local control panel only accepts connections from your own computer by default. Because your data is stored on your computer, keeping that computer secure keeps your data secure.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Disconnect any account in the app, or revoke access in that platform's settings.
  • Delete individual clips in the app, or delete ClipperBOT's data folder to remove everything.
  • Turn off the Claude clip judge to keep all clip analysis on your computer.

Since we don't hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; if you have a question, ask at the link above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
