ClipperBOT

Stream highlights, clipped for you.

ClipperBOT is a desktop app for Windows and macOS for streamers and clip editors. It watches the Twitch streams you pick, spots the moments worth sharing (big reactions, fails, clutch plays, funny bits), and turns them into vertical clips with captions. You review every clip, and only the ones you approve are uploaded to your own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook account.

  • Runs on your own computer: no account with us, no cloud storage of your clips.
  • You approve what gets posted; automatic posting is off by default.
  • Optional AI clip judge using your own Anthropic API key.
